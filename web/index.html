    How to read these measurements
    Response time runs from the scheduled arrival to the completed response, including application waiting. First-token time includes the same wait. Ollama reports input processing and generation time separately; remaining time is not a direct measurement of its queue.
    Throughput is correct reports per minute over this run. The cost estimate is run time × your assumed hourly price, divided by correct reports. It excludes warmup, idle capacity, and electricity; it is not a bill.
    All completed runs retain errors and incorrect outputs. Fewer than 20 reports give only a small-sample view of a 95th percentile. Repeated identical prompts can reuse cached computation.
